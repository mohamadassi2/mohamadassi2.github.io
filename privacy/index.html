Ampere privacy

Ampere is an independent app and is not affiliated with Tesla, Inc.

Your car data stays on your iPhone

Trips, charging sessions, costs and settings are stored on your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch and widgets through the app's shared storage. Ampere has no account system and no database of users.

Your Tesla sign-in

You sign in on Tesla's own page. Ampere keeps the resulting sign-in in your iPhone's keychain. To finish the sign-in, the one-time code is exchanged with Tesla, either directly by the app or through Ampere's small sign-in service, which only adds Ampere's app credentials and doesn't store or log codes or tokens. You can see or remove Ampere's access at any time in your Tesla account.

Your Phone Key

The key that controls your car is created in your iPhone's Secure Enclave and never leaves it. No server has a copy. You can remove it from the car on the car's screen (Controls, then Locks).

What goes to Tesla

Ampere talks to Tesla's Fleet API and to your car over Bluetooth to show your car's status and send the commands you choose. Tesla's own privacy policy covers that data. For weather alerts, Ampere asks a public weather service (Open-Meteo) for the forecast at the car's location, without any account or identifier.

No ads, no tracking

Ampere has no analytics and no advertising, and it doesn't sell or share your data.
